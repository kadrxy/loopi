# Icons

App icons come from the **Solar** icon set by 480 Design, licensed under
[CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/). Credit is shown in Settings.
They are generated into `src/ui/icon-data.ts` by `scripts/build-icons.mjs` (source: `@iconify-json/solar`).

The Apple and Google marks in `scripts/brand-paths.json` are the companies' trademarks, used only on
their sign-in buttons. The paths came from Phosphor Icons (MIT).
